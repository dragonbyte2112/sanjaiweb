Our Journey

How We Got Here

2022

The Beginning

A handful of security enthusiasts started meeting online to share knowledge and solve CTF challenges together.

2023

Growing the Community

DragonByte officially formed, hosting its first workshops and welcoming members from around the world.

2024

First Major CTF

Launched our first large-scale Capture the Flag event, drawing hundreds of participants.

2025

Open Source Projects

Members began building and shipping real security tools together as community-driven projects.

2026

Today & Beyond

Thousands of members strong, with new events, projects, and challenges added every month.
